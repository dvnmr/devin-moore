About

Start at the input. Compare it to spec.

I came up the way good troubleshooters do: my stepfather, a military calibration instructor, put electricity in front of me at sixteen and taught me to find faults systematically — understand the symptom before you pick the remedy, follow the fault until the evidence says stop. That discipline runs everything I do, on a ladder and at a keyboard.

Today I do residential and light commercial electrical work across the Mississippi Gulf Coast through Moore Electric — panels, EV chargers, generators, surge protection, troubleshooting — with written quotes and real photos of real jobs.

In parallel, I build computer systems with an AI crew I direct: a continuity system so no work is ever lost between tools, a deterministic validator that decides what counts as verified, and experiments that run on real quantum hardware. Same rule in both worlds: a claim is worth exactly the evidence under it.
